Privacy

Last updated: 10 November 2026. This site is run by Maksym Fitsak (Fitsak Studio), based in Czechia. There are no accounts and no backend of my own. The only place where you can send me personal details is the request forms (free audit and free consultation), described below.

Audit and consultation forms

If you send the free audit form or the free website consultation form, I receive: your name, your website address (optional in the consultation form), your email, Telegram, Viber or phone number (whichever you enter), the optional message, the package you chose, the page language, a request number and, if you arrived from an ad or a campaign link, its source (UTM tags and the Google click ID). The consultation form also sends a short description of your business (optional) and the way you prefer to talk: Telegram, Viber, call or email.

I use this only to reply to your request and to prepare the audit or the consultation. The legal basis is your request: steps taken at your request before a possible contract. You also tick a consent box before sending.

The form is sent through Web3Forms, a service that forwards it to my email; it handles the data only to deliver the message. I keep requests in my mailbox for up to 12 months, or delete them earlier if you ask. To have your data corrected or deleted, write to me via the contact section on the main page.

Analytics receives only the package, the form type (audit or consultation), the request number and the traffic source of a sent form. It never receives your name, website, contact, business description or message.

Analytics

I use Google Analytics 4 to understand which pages and buttons are useful. It records things like page views, clicks on buttons and links, how far you scroll, the page language, and approximate location and device type. I do not send names, emails or other personal data to it.

The site uses Google Consent Mode v2. By default analytics and advertising storage are denied. If you press Reject, no cookies are set. Nothing is loaded from Google until you accept.

If you press Accept, Google Analytics and Google Ads conversion measurement set cookies in your browser:

CookiePurposeLifetime
_gaTells one visitor from anotherup to 2 years
_ga_<ID>Keeps session stateup to 2 years
_gcl_auGoogle Ads: measures conversions after an ad clickup to 90 days

After Accept, ad storage and ad user data are allowed for conversion measurement only; ad personalisation stays denied. Before you accept, Google may still add ad click information (such as a click ID) to links inside this site so that conversions can be measured without cookies.

Session recording

I may switch on Microsoft Clarity (heatmaps and anonymised session replays) in the future. If I do, it will load only after you accept, and this page will be updated.

What is stored in your browser

  • fs_consent — your Accept/Reject choice (localStorage).
  • fs_internal — set only in my own browsers, to exclude my visits from the statistics.
  • fs_utm_source, fs_utm_medium, fs_utm_campaign, fs_utm_content, fs_utm_term, fs_gclid — campaign tags from the link you arrived with, kept in sessionStorage until you close the tab and attached to the audit form if you send it.
  • fs_debug — set only in my own browsers while I test analytics.

Change or withdraw your consent

Use the link here or in the footer of any page and choose again. To remove cookies already set, clear this site’s data in your browser settings.

Questions

Write to me via the contact section on the main page.
