FAQ

Questions I hear most

Is the consultation really free?

Yes. It doesn’t commit you to anything: you get a plan and a price, and the decision is yours.

Can I edit the texts myself?

The site is built in plain code, without a builder, so I make the edits: small ones quickly, regular ones within support.

How much does it cost to keep the site running?

Hosting for a site like this is usually free or cheap. You pay for the domain yourself at the registrar, and it stays yours.

What do I need to provide?

A logo, photos, contacts and a short description of your services. If you don’t have copy, I’ll help write it.

Why not Tilda or WordPress?

Faster loading, no monthly builder subscription, and the code and domain belong to you.

How do I pay?

50% before the start, 50% after launch.
