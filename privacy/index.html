Privacy

Last updated: 26 October 2026. This site is run by Maksym Fitsak (Fitsak Studio), based in Czechia. There are no accounts, no forms and no backend: I do not collect your name, email or any other personal details through this site.

Analytics

I use Google Analytics 4 to understand which pages and buttons are useful. It records things like page views, clicks on buttons and links, how far you scroll, the page language, and approximate location and device type. I do not send names, emails or other personal data to it.

The site uses Google Consent Mode v2. By default analytics storage is denied. If you press Reject, no analytics cookies are set. Nothing is loaded from Google until you accept.

If you press Accept, Google Analytics sets cookies in your browser:

CookiePurposeLifetime
_gaTells one visitor from anotherup to 2 years
_ga_<ID>Keeps session stateup to 2 years

Advertising storage and personalisation are always denied on this site.

Session recording

I may switch on Microsoft Clarity (heatmaps and anonymised session replays) in the future. If I do, it will load only after you accept, and this page will be updated.

What is stored in your browser

  • fs_consent — your Accept/Reject choice (localStorage).
  • fs_internal — set only in my own browsers, to exclude my visits from the statistics.

Change or withdraw your consent

Use the link here or in the footer of any page and choose again. To remove cookies already set, clear this site’s data in your browser settings.
