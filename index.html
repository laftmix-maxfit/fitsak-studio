Case study

NONA — online guitar school

Website, analytics and search ads from scratch.

The goal

The school needed trial-lesson sign-ups from the web: a site people trust, ads that bring the right people, and analytics that show where students come from.

What I did

  • Built the site from scratch: home page, lessons landing page, dedicated pages for kids, adults, beginners and “first guitar”; student, teacher and admin dashboards.
  • GA4 analytics with ~20 custom events: every step is tracked, from opening the form to sending it or tapping Telegram.
  • Google Search Ads: launched the campaign, added 100+ negative keywords to cut irrelevant clicks, rewrote the ad around real search queries.
  • Cut the sign-up form down to 4 fields and added Telegram / Viber buttons for people who’d rather message.
  • SEO: dedicated landing pages for key searches, Search Console, sitemap.

Tracking examples

What a setup looks like

Online store

Where do shoppers drop off between seeing a product and paying?

  1. view_itemopens a product page
  2. add_to_cartadds it to the cart
  3. begin_checkoutstarts checkout
  4. purchasepays: order value and ID

Drop-off is the gap between any two steps in a GA4 funnel report.

Booking & service business

Which channels bring bookings, and where do people give up on the form?

  1. cta_clickclicks “Book”
  2. form_openthe booking form opens
  3. form_fieldfills in a field (field name only, never the value)
  4. generate_leadsends the request

Also tracked: phone_click and messenger_click, for people who call or write instead.

Landing page with ads

Which ads and keywords bring real leads, not just clicks?

  1. utm / gclidarrives from an ad, source is kept
  2. scroll_depthreads the page: 25, 50, 75%
  3. cta_clickclicks the main button
  4. generate_leadsends the form with lead_id to match it in your CRM

Examples of setups I build. Real project: see the NONA case above.
